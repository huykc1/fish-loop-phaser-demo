## Prompt
Using the attached gameplay screenshot as the style reference (same glossy 3D cartoon render, soft studio lighting, bright saturated colors, clean edges),
create a cute cartoon TUTORIAL HAND pointer for a casual mobile puzzle game, shown in TWO states side by side on one image.
The hand: chubby cartoon hand with light warm skin tone, a short light-blue sleeve cuff with a white trim at the wrist, soft darker outline,
the INDEX FINGER extended and pointing straight UP (fingertip at the top), the other fingers curled into the palm, thumb tucked, palm facing the viewer, wrist at the bottom.
LEFT half: the hand RAISED (relaxed, index finger fully extended, pointing up).
RIGHT half: the SAME hand in the PRESSED / tapping state: same angle and size, index finger pressed slightly bent and shorter as if pushing a button, hand a little squashed.
Both hands must have the same size, angle and position inside their half, centered, whole hand and cuff visible, with clear empty space around them.
Plain flat pure green (#00FF00) background everywhere, no shadow, no motion lines, no tap ripples, no text. Landscape image 1536x1024.

## Lần 2 (designer góp ý: tay hướng dẫn chỉ nên thấy mu bàn tay)
Redo [hand-pointer] with one change: show the BACK of the hand (the dorsal side) facing the viewer, like a classic mobile game tutorial tap-hint hand.
The index finger points straight UP; the other three fingers are folded down so only the back of those fingers and their knuckles are visible — no palm, no fingertips or nails of the folded fingers visible; the thumb is tucked against the side of the hand.
Keep everything else the same: same glossy 3D cartoon style as the gameplay reference, same light warm skin tone, short light-blue sleeve cuff with white trim at the wrist, soft darker outline.
LEFT half: hand RAISED (index finger fully extended). RIGHT half: the SAME hand PRESSED (index finger slightly bent and shorter as if tapping, hand a little squashed).
Both hands same size, angle and position in their half, centered, whole hand and cuff visible. Plain flat pure green (#00FF00) background, no shadow, no motion lines, no ripples, no text. Landscape image 1536x1024.
