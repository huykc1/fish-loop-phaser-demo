## Prompt
Using the attached gameplay screenshot and clownfish sheet as the style reference (glossy 3D cartoon, bright saturated, casual mobile puzzle game),
design the GAME LOGO for a mobile puzzle game named exactly "FISH LOOP" (two words, spelled F-I-S-H  L-O-O-P).
Big chunky rounded 3D bubble letters, white-to-light-blue glossy fill with a thick deep purple (#4b2bb5) outline and a soft dark navy drop shadow.
The two "O" letters of LOOP are drawn as a transparent glass water-pipe ring with water inside; one cute orange clownfish (same style as the reference fish) swims out of the second O, plus a few small bubbles.
Centered, whole logo visible with empty space around it, no other text, no tagline.
Do NOT use any green color anywhere in the logo. Plain flat pure green (#00FF00) background, no shadow on the background, no frame. Landscape image 1536x1024.
