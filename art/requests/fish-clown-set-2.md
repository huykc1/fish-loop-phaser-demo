## Prompt
Using the attached sprite sheet of 8 clownfish as the exact reference, create 7 MORE clownfish in the IDENTICAL style, shape, pose, size and camera angle (side view facing RIGHT, chunky rounded body, two stripes with thin dark outlines, big friendly eye, small fins with darker edges). Only the body color changes.
Arrange them in a grid of 4 columns x 2 rows, evenly spaced with clear gaps, each fish centered in its cell, whole fish visible. Leave the LAST cell (row 2, column 4) completely empty.
Row 1 (left to right): BROWN (#8b5a2b, stripes white), WHITE (#f4f4f4 body, stripes light grey #b9c3cc), BLACK (#2b2b33 body, stripes white), GREY (#9aa0a8, stripes white).
Row 2 (left to right): SKY BLUE (#7fd0ff, stripes white), LIME (#b8f23a, stripes white), NAVY (#1f2f8f, stripes white).
Plain flat solid MAGENTA (#FF00FF) background everywhere, no shadows, no text, no labels, no borders. Landscape image 1536x1024.

Màu chọn để khác rõ 8 màu cũ (đỏ, vàng, xanh lá, xanh dương, tím, cam, hồng, cyan).
